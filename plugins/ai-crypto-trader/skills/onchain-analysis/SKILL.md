# On-chain Analysis
Identify chain, asset/contract, metric provider, time window, and timestamp. Distinguish raw, adjusted, estimated, and provider-labelled metrics. Review exchange netflows, active addresses, transaction counts, realized capitalization, holder concentration, and large transfers only when available. Compare metrics with historical baselines and price action. Explain alternative interpretations: exchange flows and whale transfers do not prove intent to buy or sell.

Output metric, observed change, source/time, possible interpretation, caveats, and evidence that could confirm or weaken the thesis. Never invent wallet identities or attribution.
