# Trade Journal
Preserve timestamp, venue, symbol, market type, timeframe, thesis, entry, stop, target, sizing assumptions, fees, and exit reason. Separate signal outcomes from executed paper positions. Review expectancy, win rate, average win/loss, drawdown, and rule adherence only with sufficient samples. Never delete losing examples or rewrite the original thesis retrospectively.
