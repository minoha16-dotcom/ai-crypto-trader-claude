# Portfolio Allocation
Request holdings, valuation timestamp, cost basis if relevant, and constraints. Measure concentration by asset, sector, stablecoin, chain, and exchange where data permits. Highlight correlated positions and liquidity risks. Do not prescribe personalized investment allocations; provide scenario analysis and explain assumptions. Keep cash and collateral treatment explicit.
