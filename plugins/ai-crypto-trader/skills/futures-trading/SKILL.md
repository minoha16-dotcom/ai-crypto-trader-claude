# Futures Trading Research
Require exchange, contract symbol, linear/inverse type, isolated/cross margin, leverage, side, entry, stop, and contract specification. Check funding interval, open interest, basis, volume, and liquidation data only when sourced and timestamped. Do not treat funding or open interest alone as directional signals. Model adverse movement, fees, funding, maintenance margin, and liquidation using actual venue rules. Compare with Spot. Never recommend high leverage or invent liquidation prices/multipliers. No live orders or credential collection.
