# Derivatives Analysis
Use venue-specific, timestamped data. Explain funding interval and sign convention, open-interest units, contract type, and basis calculation. Look for confirmation across price, volume, funding, and OI; avoid simplistic contrarian claims. Liquidation maps are estimates, not guaranteed execution levels. State data gaps and venue differences.
