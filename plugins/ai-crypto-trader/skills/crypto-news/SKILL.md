# Crypto News Research
Prioritize primary sources: project announcements, exchange notices, regulators, court filings, and official data. Record publication time, event time, source, and confirmation status. Cross-check consequential claims with independent reputable sources where possible. Separate confirmed facts, reported claims, speculation, and interpretation. Explain possible impact channels without asserting a price outcome.

Do not invent headlines, dates, quotes, or sources. Flag recycled, sponsored, anonymous, and unverified social claims.
