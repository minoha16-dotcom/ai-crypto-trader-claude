# Strategy Backtesting
Specify rules, venue, market type, candle interval, sample window, and data source. Use chronological train/test splits, realistic fees, spread, slippage, funding, and delisted assets where possible. Avoid look-ahead bias, data leakage, and parameter overfitting. Report trade count, exposure, drawdown, expectancy, and benchmark comparison. Backtests are not forecasts; disclose limitations.
